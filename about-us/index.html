About Us

Our story, our team, and our commitment to reliable non-emergency medical transportation.

Our Story

One less thing to worry about

Corporate Livery set out to solve a problem: getting to a medical appointment shouldn't be the hardest part of someone's day. We built our company around dependable, comfortable rides for people navigating mobility challenges, so a lack of transportation is never the reason someone misses the care they need.

Who We Serve

A trusted ride for every client

Our clients include Medicare and Medicaid beneficiaries as well as those paying privately, all part of the community we're proud to serve.

Our Team

Drivers who put you first

Every driver and coordinator on our team is trained and experienced in non-emergency medical transport. They know the routes, the schedules, and what it takes to make each passenger feel comfortable, treating every trip as more than just a drop-off.

Our Mission

Care doesn't end at pickup

We measure success one ride at a time: getting people to their appointments safely, on schedule, and with genuine care along the way. Transportation is often an overlooked piece of healthcare, but for our clients, a dependable ride can make all the difference in staying on top of their health.
